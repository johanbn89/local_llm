# Source Quality

Use these checks when comparing research sources or judging evidence.

## Stronger Sources

- Peer-reviewed papers.
- Official datasets or technical reports.
- Reputable institutions with transparent methodology.
- Recent sources when the field changes quickly.

## Weaker Sources

- Unsourced blog posts.
- Marketing pages.
- Commentary that does not link to primary evidence.
- Old sources in fast-changing fields.

## Red Flags

- No author or organization listed.
- Claims that are stronger than the data.
- Missing methodology.
- Conflicts of interest that are not disclosed.

## Reporting Uncertainty

Use plain language:

- "The evidence is mixed."
- "The sources agree on X but differ on Y."
- "This appears plausible, but the available sources are indirect."
